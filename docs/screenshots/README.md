# Job Copilot, step by step

A tour of the app, one screenshot per step. Apart from the sign-in page, the screenshots come from the app's public demo, which uses made-up sample jobs, people and kits. They show no real data.

**Try it yourself:** [job-search-copilot-blue.vercel.app/demo](https://job-search-copilot-blue.vercel.app/demo). No sign-in is needed, and nothing is saved.

## 1. Sign in

Invited people sign in with an emailed link or with Google. People who haven't been invited can't get in.

![Sign-in page](01-sign-in.png)

## 2. Your board

Every job is a card. Drag a card from **Wishlist** to **Applied**, **Interviewing**, **Offer** or **Rejected** as things move along. The **Due today** strip at the top lists today's interviews and any follow-ups that are due or overdue. Click **Done** to clear a follow-up, or **Snooze** to move it to later.

![Board](02-board.png)

## 3. Add a job from a link

Click **Add job** and paste the job posting's link. The AI reads the page for you.

![Add a job from a link](03-add-job-link.png)

## 4. Check the details

The AI fills in the title, company, location, salary and work style. Change anything that's wrong, choose a column, then click **Add job**.

![Check the details](04-add-job-review.png)

## 5. The job is on your board

![Job added](05-job-added.png)

## 6. Or paste the description

Some sites, such as LinkedIn and Indeed, block reading by link. On those, copy the job description and paste it in the **Paste text** tab instead.

![Paste the job description](06-add-job-paste-text.png)

## 7. Or type it in yourself

![Type the details in](07-add-job-type-it-in.png)

## 8. Open a job

Click a card to open its panel. From there you can:

- change the column or the star priority
- set a follow-up reminder
- record the date you applied
- write notes
- read the saved job description

![Job details](08-job-details.png)

## 9. Interviews and contacts

Add interview dates and times (they appear in **Due today** on the day) and the people you're talking to.

![Interviews and contacts](09-job-interviews-contacts.png)

## 10. Application kit

Open the **Application kit** tab. The kit is written from your resume and the job description, and the panel shows how many kits you have left this month.

![Start a kit](10-kit-start.png)

## 11. The kit writes itself

All four parts are written at once. This takes about a minute, and you can close the panel while it works.

![Kit being written](11-kit-writing.png)

## 12. Cover letter

Each part can be copied, edited or downloaded.

![Cover letter](12-kit-ready.png)

## 13. Company brief

A one-page brief based on a live web search. It covers what the company does, recent news, its culture and smart questions to ask.

![Company brief](13-kit-company-brief.png)

## 14. Download

Download the whole kit, or a single part, as a Word document or a PDF.

![Download as Word or PDF](14-kit-download.png)

## 15. Settings

Add your name, which is used to sign your cover letters. Upload your resume once, as a PDF or Word file, or paste it as text.

![Settings](15-settings.png)

## 16. Admin (app owner only)

Invite people by email and see how many kits each person has used and what they cost. Click a kit count to change that person's monthly limit.

![Admin](16-admin.png)

## 17. Dark mode

Use the sun/moon button to switch between light and dark.

![Dark mode](17-board-dark.png)

## 18. On your phone

The app works in a phone's browser too. Swipe sideways to see the other columns.

![Phone](18-board-phone.png)
